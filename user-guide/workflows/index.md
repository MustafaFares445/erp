# Business Workflows

These guides explain how work moves across IERP modules from start to finish. Use a workflow guide when your goal is to complete a business process rather than manage one record in isolation.

<div class="module-grid">
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/quote-to-cash"><strong>Quote to Cash</strong><span>Complete a customer sale from the first commercial offer through confirmed demand, warehouse execution, invoicing, collection and accounting.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/customer-return-to-refund"><strong>Customer Return to Refund</strong><span>Handle the physical return and its financial consequence without mixing Inventory and Accounting responsibilities.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/procure-to-pay"><strong>Procure to Pay</strong><span>Buy from a supplier through sourcing, Purchase Order commitment, supplier confirmation, Inventory receipt, supplier liability and payment.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/sales-shortage-procurement"><strong>Sales Shortage Procurement</strong><span>Cover a Sales Order shortage through purchasing and bring received stock back into fulfillment readiness.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/warehouse-replenishment"><strong>Warehouse Replenishment</strong><span>Turn min/max replenishment policy and stock alerts into controlled purchase needs and supplier orders.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/inventory-transfer"><strong>Inventory Transfer</strong><span>Move stock between warehouse locations while preserving source, destination and tracking identity.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/physical-count"><strong>Physical Count</strong><span>Compare physical stock with system stock and post an approved variance through the controlled count workflow.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/inventory-correction"><strong>Inventory Correction</strong><span>Correct a previously posted inventory operation with an explicit historical correction instead of rewriting the original movement.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/tracked-inventory"><strong>Tracked Inventory Lifecycle</strong><span>Follow lot-, serial- and expiry-controlled inventory from receipt through reservation, delivery and customer custody.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/lead-to-sale"><strong>Lead to Sale</strong><span>Move potential business from a lead through opportunity and customer context into the formal Sales lifecycle.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/customer-pricing"><strong>Customer Pricing</strong><span>Understand how customer profile, currency, price lists, tiers, quantity breaks and floor controls influence selling price.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/accounts-receivable"><strong>Accounts Receivable</strong><span>Follow customer invoiced value through receivable status, payment allocation, settlement, credit and write-off controls.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/accounts-payable"><strong>Accounts Payable</strong><span>Follow supplier billing from received purchasing work into payable liability and supplier payment.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/period-close"><strong>Fiscal Period Review and Close</strong><span>Review posted financial activity and control whether new transactions can affect a fiscal period.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/employee-field-sales"><strong>Employee Field Sales</strong><span>Execute an employee plan through tasks and visits, capture outcomes, follow-up and approved sales opportunities, then continue into Sales.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/employee-performance"><strong>Employee Performance and Salary</strong><span>Turn completed operational evidence into performance review and salary-calculation context.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/support-case"><strong>Support Case Lifecycle</strong><span>Handle a customer ticket from intake and triage through SLA, assignment, work, customer waiting, resolution and closure.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/maintenance"><strong>Maintenance and Repair</strong><span>Coordinate equipment diagnosis, approval, repair, quality checks and service evidence.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/warranty"><strong>Warranty Service</strong><span>Evaluate equipment entitlement and execute covered service while preserving warranty evidence and authorized corrections.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/field-service"><strong>Field Service</strong><span>Schedule and execute a customer service appointment with the correct equipment, assignee and service outcome.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/workflows/equipment-lifecycle"><strong>Customer Equipment Lifecycle</strong><span>Follow serialized equipment from Inventory delivery into customer custody, support, maintenance, loaner or supplier repair and later service history.</span></a>

## Core rule

Every step is completed in the module that owns it. Do not use manual status changes or direct data edits to bypass a cross-module transition.
