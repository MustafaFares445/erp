# Getting Started

IERP is organized around **nine business modules**. Each module owns a specific part of the business, while end-to-end workflows connect them.

<IerpPageSummary purpose="Learn the dashboard language and operating rules before working with business records." useWhen="You are new to IERP or need to understand how navigation, statuses, tables and permissions behave." owner="All dashboard users" />

## Sign in

Open the administration dashboard and enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**. Leave **Remember me** unchecked on a shared computer. After signing in, use the module switcher to open the area your role allows you to use.

## The ownership rule

IERP deliberately separates responsibilities:

- **Sales** owns customer commercial documents.
- **Purchasing** owns supplier commercial commitments.
- **Inventory** owns physical stock truth.
- **Accounting** owns ledger truth.
- **Payments** records money movement and allocation.
- **CRM** owns customer relationship context.
- **Employees** owns planning, visits and performance.
- **Support & Maintenance** owns service coordination.
- **Reports** reads business facts but does not repair them.

::: warning
Do not bypass a workflow by manually changing a status or editing a posted quantity/value. Use the dedicated action, correction, reversal, return, credit or refund path.
:::

## What this guide covers

Every routable administration screen is represented by a screen article. Each article explains:

- navigation path;
- list/create/view/edit pages;
- fields and form controls discovered from the current implementation;
- table columns, filters and search;
- lifecycle actions;
- related tabs;
- business effect;
- common blocked states;
- screenshot placeholders for every real page.

Verified screenshots appear beside the instructions they explain. Screens that have not yet been photographed retain a text reference.
