# Sales

Sales controls the customer commercial lifecycle from quotation and confirmed demand through fulfillment, billing, collection and credit correction.

## How to use this module

Start from the module dashboard to understand queues and exceptions, then open the screen that owns the record or action you need. The structure below follows the dashboard module switcher and sidebar.

## Dashboard screens

<div class="module-grid">
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/sales/dashboard"><strong>Sales Dashboard</strong><span>Use the Sales Dashboard to understand current selling activity, customer/salesperson performance, commercial queues and the records that need attention before opening detailed Sales pages.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/sales/quotation"><strong>Quotations</strong><span>Create, send, review and convert customer quotations while preserving pricing, validity and customer-decision history before demand becomes a Sales Order.</span></a>

## Ownership rule

Sales may create or update downstream fulfillment, invoice, payment and accounting work. Stock changes only through Inventory actions.
