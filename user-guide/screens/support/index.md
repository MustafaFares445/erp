# Support and Maintenance

Support & Maintenance coordinates customer tickets, SLA, warranty, service work, maintenance, field appointments, equipment context and advanced routing.

## How to use this module

Start from the module dashboard to understand queues and exceptions, then open the screen that owns the record or action you need. The structure below follows the dashboard module switcher and sidebar.

## Ownership rule

Support coordinates service work and can trigger maintenance or chargeable commercial follow-up, but stock custody and ledger posting remain owned by Inventory and Accounting.
