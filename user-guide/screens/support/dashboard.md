# Support dashboard: current service priorities

<IerpPageSummary purpose="Prioritize support tickets, warranty responsibilities, maintenance work, calendar-driven visits and equipment requiring attention." useWhen="At the beginning of a support shift or when coordinating team workload." owner="Support and Maintenance" />

## When to use this page

At the beginning of a support shift or when coordinating team workload.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Start each support shift” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “Financial and escalation boundaries” instructions below.

## What the overview shows

<IerpScreenshot id="support-dashboard-overview" title="Support dashboard" description="Compare active case counts, trends, service economics and the ticket and maintenance queues that need attention." path="Support and Maintenance → Dashboard" imageSrc="/images/en/support/support-dashboard-overview.png" />

The dashboard combines ticket KPI cards, recent trends, case queues, maintenance that needs attention and upcoming scheduled work. Depending on enabled features, operational queues also flag **due/overdue/failed calibration**, **overdue loaners** and **equipment at suppliers**. Their presence does not grant permission to modify linked records.

Use employee and priority filters to focus a working set. Dashboard counts and slices may use different scope or time windows; read each label before comparing totals. Filter changes do not assign a ticket or complete a service task.

## Start each support shift

1. Open cases blocked by missing triage, payment or customer information and confirm the next authorized action.
2. Prioritize SLA at-risk or breached tickets, then inspect response and resolution clocks in the actual ticket.
3. Check customer appointments and technician availability; do not assume a visit is complete because it was scheduled.
4. Open maintenance awaiting customer quotation approval, QA/rework or coverage decision. A partially covered job should not be started before the appropriate accepted quotation.
5. Review upcoming preventive/calibration work and overdue equipment. Use the linked request, not dashboard counters, to perform changes.

## Financial and escalation boundaries

A warranty-eligible unit does not guarantee this repair is fully covered. The maintenance request records diagnosis and customer share, Sales handles quotations/invoices, Payments handles settlement and Accounting handles posting. Dashboard revenue or cost summaries are reporting information, not proof of payment.

For disputes or unexpected KPI counts, open the linked ticket or report and inspect its underlying period, status and audit history. Preserve history rather than changing a case merely to adjust a chart.
