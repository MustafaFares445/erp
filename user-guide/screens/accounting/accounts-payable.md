# Accounts payable and supplier aging

<IerpPageSummary purpose="Inspect approved supplier bill obligations, aging and actual settlement." useWhen="Reviewing what the company owes suppliers and prioritizing payment." owner="Accounting" />

## When to use this page

Reviewing what the company owes suppliers and prioritizing payment.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Reconciliation procedure” instructions below.
<IerpScreenshot id="accounting-accounts-payable-index" title="Supplier payable balances" description="Outstanding approved bills and aging." imageSrc="/images/en/accounting/accounting-accounts-payable-index.png" />

The AP list reflects approved/posted supplier liabilities; it is not an alternate way to create a purchase order, receive stock or issue a bank payment.

## Reconciliation procedure

1. Filter by supplier or due date and inspect the bill, its approval status, currency and current outstanding amount.
2. Follow the Bill detail to compare approved lines and source purchase order; Inventory receiving remains independent.
3. Record a Draft Supplier Payment and allocate it only to eligible bills of that supplier. Paying it creates the approved ledger and settlement effects.
4. Revisit AP after a partial payment and final settlement; differences must be traced to source bills, postings or allocations.

Reports and aging filters are read-only. Do not edit ledger rows to force the AP balance to match.
