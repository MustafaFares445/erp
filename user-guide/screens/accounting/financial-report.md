# Financial reports — read-only ledger analysis

<IerpPageSummary purpose="Inspect trial balance, general ledger, profit and loss, balance sheet and posting register from posted accounting facts." useWhen="Reconcile a period, audit postings or prepare financial statements." owner="Accounting" />

## When to use this page

Reconcile a period, audit postings or prepare financial statements.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “How to use” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “Interpreting differences” instructions below.

<IerpScreenshot id="accounting-financial-report-index" title="Financial reports" description="Choose a report type and filters; results are read-only." imageSrc="/images/en/accounting/accounting-financial-report-index.png" />

## How to use

1. Open **Financial Reports**, then select **Trial Balance**, **General Ledger**, **Profit and Loss**, **Balance Sheet** or **Posting Register**.
2. Set the fiscal period or the relevant From/To range; for the Balance Sheet use the As of date. General Ledger may also filter by account.
3. Review the currency, date boundary and account balances. Compare totals with source journal entries and the tax register.
4. Export with the page's permitted action if needed. Exporting and viewing must not post, correct, close or delete journal entries.

## Interpreting differences

A report is a projection of recorded postings, not a correction workflow. Investigate unbalanced or missing source activity through the originating Sales, Purchasing, Payments or Accounting record and apply an authorized correction/reversal. Do not modify report totals directly.
