# Employees

Employees covers workforce records, monthly planning, tasks, field visits, reviewed sales opportunities, performance and salary calculation.

## How to use this module

Start from the module dashboard to understand queues and exceptions, then open the screen that owns the record or action you need. The structure below follows the dashboard module switcher and sidebar.

Employee activity contributes to field execution and performance while warehouse, pricing and accounting responsibilities remain in their owning modules.
