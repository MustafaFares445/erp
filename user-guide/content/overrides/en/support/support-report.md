## Select a report section

<IerpScreenshot id="support-support-report-index" title="Support reports" description="Choose the operational section, date range and optional CSV export within your report permissions." path="Support and Maintenance → Support Reports" imageSrc="/images/en/support/support-support-report-index.png" />

The sections include **Overview**, **Service Desk**, **Workload**, **Field Service**, **Reliability & Warranty**, **Equipment Lifecycle**, **Product Quality**, **Financial** and **Preventive**. Most sections accept date filters; Workload intentionally reflects the current backlog instead of a historical period.

Use the same date range before comparing trends. A ticket's response and resolution metrics measure SLA milestone timing, while an internal note does not count as a customer response. Field service shows technician utilization and maintenance work, but scheduled time must not be mistaken for actual time on-site.

## Financial and warranty interpretation

The Financial section summarizes recorded service cost, recognized service revenue and job margin. A positive margin figure is not proof of a settled customer payment: Sales invoices, Payments transactions and Accounting ledger postings remain separate owners. Warranty Recovery tracks claims, receipts and remaining amounts independently from customer-funded revenue.

Equipment Lifecycle includes installation/commissioning, calibration due/failed, loaners and supplier repair data where those capabilities are enabled. A hidden or empty optional section may reflect a feature gate or genuine absence of records, not a completed lifecycle.

## Export and investigate

**Export Current Report** produces a permission-controlled CSV of the selected report section. Check the filters, currency and evidence dates before sharing it; it may contain customer, equipment or cost data. Exports and filters are read-only and cannot settle a charge, post inventory movements or close tickets.

When numbers appear inconsistent, follow the drill-down to the owning ticket or maintenance request and compare its status with source financial documents. Do not manually adjust report values to force them to match a desired outcome.
