# Knowledge article categories

<IerpPageSummary purpose="Organize approved support guidance into discoverable categories while controlling each category's active status." useWhen="Knowledge articles need consistent grouping for the Support team and customer help search." owner="Support and Maintenance" />

## When to use this page

Knowledge articles need consistent grouping for the Support team and customer help search.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Create and maintain” instructions below.

## Availability and articles

Knowledge Categories share the optional Knowledge Base rollout and model permissions. A category is not a customer-visible article. Article publishing and visibility remain separate authorization and editorial steps.

<IerpScreenshot id="support-knowledge-article-category-index" title="Knowledge categories" description="Check category name, slug, active status and article count before choosing a category." path="Advanced Support → Knowledge Categories" />

## Create and maintain

<IerpScreenshot id="support-knowledge-article-category-create" title="New knowledge category" description="Choose a clear name, unique slug and deliberate active state." path="Knowledge Categories → Create" />

Use stable, readable slugs and non-overlapping category names. Check existing articles to avoid fragmenting closely related technical guidance.

<IerpScreenshot id="support-knowledge-article-category-edit" title="Edit a knowledge category" description="Review the impact of renaming or deactivating a category on article discoverability." path="Knowledge Categories → Edit" />

Deactivating a category prevents its normal selection on new article forms. Do not assume that changing category status automatically unpublishes all articles or revokes previously shared content; use article-level visibility and publishing controls for those purposes.
