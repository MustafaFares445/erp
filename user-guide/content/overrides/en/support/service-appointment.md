# Field service appointments: technician dispatch and evidence

## When to use this page

Work requires a visit for repair, installation, inspection or calibration.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Plan the visit” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “From dispatch to completion” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “Investigate blockers” instructions below.

## Plan the visit

Field Service appointments are linked to a service task/maintenance request. They do not replace the approval, warranty decision or technician execution record. Review customer location, appointment purpose, assigned employee, working window and existing dispatch conflicts.

<IerpScreenshot id="support-service-appointment-index" title="Field service appointments" description="Review assigned visits, dispatch status, customer/equipment context and the corresponding job." path="Support and Maintenance → Field Service" />

## From dispatch to completion

A visit for installation or calibration draws its work purpose from the owning maintenance request, not from a separate duplicated checklist. Only after real travel/check-in and completed work should a visit be marked finished with required evidence or sign-off. Failure to gain customer access or a failed service inspection is not a successful visit.

Technician time and parts spent belong to the linked service records; extra customer charges need the parent request's quote/coverage approval. Closing an appointment does not automatically certify calibration, activate warranty or settle a Sales invoice.

## Investigate blockers

For an unavailable agent, check schedule overlap and employment/permission eligibility. For an incomplete visit, review missing evidence, planned versus actual times and the equipment details. Reassign or reschedule with an audit trail rather than marking a failed visit as complete.
