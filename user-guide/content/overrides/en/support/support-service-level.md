# Support service levels: contractual tiers

<IerpPageSummary purpose="Define a stable named level of support service that can be granted to customers and targeted by SLA policies." useWhen="A support contract differentiates Standard, Premium or other documented service tiers." owner="Support and Maintenance" />

## When to use this page

A support contract differentiates Standard, Premium or other documented service tiers.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Service levels are definitions, not grants” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “Create and manage” instructions below.

## Service levels are definitions, not grants

A service level is a contractual tier with a unique stable code, descriptive name and active status. A customer does **not** receive that level merely because it exists in the catalog; a separate Support Entitlement grants it for a defined scope and period.

## Create and manage

<IerpScreenshot id="support-support-service-level-create" title="Create service level" description="Enter a unique integration-safe code, name, active state and explicit service commitments." path="Support Service Levels → Create" imageSrc="/images/en/support/support-support-service-level-create.png" />

Choose a short stable alphanumeric/hyphen/underscore code, meaningful name and description matching the commercial agreement. Activating a tier makes it available for use but does not attach it to every customer.

<IerpScreenshot id="support-support-service-level-edit" title="Edit service level" description="Review existing entitlements and SLA policy targeting before changing a service tier." path="Support Service Levels → Edit" imageSrc="/images/en/support/support-support-service-level-edit.png" />

Renaming or deactivating a service level can affect future selection and reporting. Existing entitlements and ticket SLA snapshots require separate evidence and handling. Do not hide a contractual obligation by disabling its tier.
