Investigate the acquisition funnel, campaign performance and open opportunity pipeline using read-only summaries of the operational CRM data. Reports require the CRM funnel-report permission.

## Run a report

1. Open **CRM Reports** and use the **Report** selector to choose the question you need answered.
2. **Leads by source** compares lead volume and conversions by acquisition channel; **Stage conversion** summarizes lead counts at each stage.
3. **Campaign performance** compares recipients, interested responses, attributed leads and opportunities across campaigns.
4. **Pipeline value and age** groups open opportunities by stage and currency, including average age and monetary pipeline value.
5. **Attributed revenue** reports the collected revenue linked to campaigns. Do not confuse attribution with issuing a new invoice or collecting a new payment.
6. Use **Export current report** to download CSV for the currently selected report. Export is read-only; no CRM state is changed.

## Reconcile differences

A zero or unexpected figure may reflect date, source status or missing attribution. Open the source lead/campaign/opportunity and verify evidence rather than editing report results. Monetary figures remain owned by the source Sales/Payments/Accounting workflows.
