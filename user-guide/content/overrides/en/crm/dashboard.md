# CRM Dashboard

## When to use this page

Use this screen to review its available records and supported actions.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Review the working picture” instructions below.

<IerpNavigationPath :items='["CRM","Overview","Dashboard"]' />

The dashboard summarizes lead follow-up, customer growth and campaign performance. These are operational indicators, not substitutes for source CRM records or Sales documents.

## Review the working picture

1. Open the CRM dashboard and set the **Date range**. If needed, choose a **Lead source** to focus the lead statistics on a particular acquisition channel.
2. Review the lead status distribution. A lead that has been converted into a customer remains part of the historical conversion funnel, not a new live prospect.
3. Compare **Customer growth** and **New leads by status** for the selected period.
4. Inspect the **Dormant leads** queue for leads lacking interaction for 14 days or more, then open the individual lead to document follow-up.
5. Inspect per-campaign delivery outcomes and attributed leads. Confirm response evidence inside the Campaign detail before attributing downstream Sales.
6. Use the relevant **Customers**, **Leads**, **Interactions**, or **Campaigns** records for corrective action. Charts and filters do not themselves approve, convert or dispatch anything.

## Important boundaries

CRM owns the relationship records and conversion context; Sales owns quotations and sales orders, Inventory owns physical returns, and Accounting owns ledger balances. Filtered dashboard counts are neither accounting balances nor a promise of stock availability.
