Review requests to change product-variant cost, markup, base price and minimum price. The history resource is an Inventory-owned pricing approval workflow, not a field for directly rewriting prices in CRM.

## Review process

1. Open **Price Change Requests** and search by product SKU or variant. Filter by status and inspect the requester, timestamp and values.
2. Compare **Cost price**, **Markup %**, **Base price** and **Minimum price** with the current variant and business constraints.
3. Requests can be **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. Authorized pricing reviewers may approve, reject or update a pending request using controlled actions.
4. **Approve** applies eligible changes through the Product Pricing service; **Reject** preserves the decision; **Update** revalidates and applies adjusted numbers.
5. A reviewed request cannot be modified or deleted. Customer selling prices may also depend on tiers, customer eligibility and a floor override.
