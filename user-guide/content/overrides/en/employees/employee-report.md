# Employee Reports

## When to use this page

Use this screen to review its available records and supported actions.

## Typical tasks

- Follow the documented “Find the right report” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “Report workspace” instructions below.
- Follow the documented “Access and limitations” instructions below.

<IerpNavigationPath :items='["Employees","Reports","Employee Reports"]' />

Review task and visit execution, performance and salaries through permission-limited reports. Each report derives from existing records; exporting results does not change operational or financial state.

## Find the right report

1. Open **Employee Reports** and choose a category available to your role: **Tasks and visits**, **Performance** or **Salary**.
2. Task/visit reports include **Plan Completion**, **Overdue Tasks** and **Unexecuted Visits** to identify gaps.
3. Performance reports include **Performance by Employee** and **Performance by Month** to compare approved calculated scores.
4. Salary reports include **Salary by Employee** and **Salary by Month**. Understand calculation state before interpreting amounts; viewing a report does not confirm salary.
5. Apply the relevant table filters and choose **Export current**. The system queues an export for the currently selected report and filters; a file may not download immediately.
6. Correct unexpected results in the owning Plan, Task, Visit, review, scoring or salary service and regenerate the report.

## Access and limitations

Reports appear according to available report permissions. A category absent for your role does not prove that the business records are missing. Report access does not imply authority to change salaries or approve bonuses.
