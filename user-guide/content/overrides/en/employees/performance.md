Review calculated employee performance against the monthly plan's tasks, visits, scheduling and human-approved opportunities. Scores are derived facts, not free-form marks to edit in the list.

## Reviewing results

1. Open **Performance** and find the employee, plan and calculation period.
2. Inspect **View** for source plan, task completion, visit requirements, work timing, opportunity factor and calculation timestamp.
3. If scores appear wrong, revisit source tasks/visits and the relevant plan's weights; use an authorized recalculation rather than editing the score.
4. Reopening a completed task marks the plan's performance evidence stale so recalculation is needed.
5. Only **Approved/Accepted** opportunities reviewed by humans count. AI **Draft** and **Rejected** evidence cannot improve an employee's score or bonus.
6. Performance does not confirm salaries: use the separate Salary Calculation workflow for confirmation and approved bonus decisions.
