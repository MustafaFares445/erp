## Approval and payment

1. Record the merchant or supplier, active postable expense account, active payment method, date, description, subtotal, tax and total. Add a supporting private PDF/image receipt if available.
2. **Approve** a Draft only with the relevant permission. Approval posts the expense liability/accounting effects through the Accounting service.
3. **Pay** an Approved expense using the payment-date confirmation. Payout adds a separate accounting effect; it is not implied by approval.
4. **Cancel draft expense** is only for Draft records. Do not delete or rewrite Paid expense history.

Expense receipts are privately stored; share them only through authorized application controls. Expenses do not receive inventory stock or settle unrelated supplier bills automatically.
