- Follow the documented “Daily review” instructions below.

## Daily review

1. Confirm the selected reporting window and read the posted journal and tax summaries.
2. Review the open fiscal period's **last measured** close checklist. Reading the dashboard does not execute the checks.
3. Open the relevant journal, period, receivable or tax screen to investigate the underlying record.
4. Apply financial corrections only from permission-controlled originating documents or reversal/approval workflows.

This dashboard is a decision surface, not an alternative posting command. Totals are not proof that a bank payment or supplier bill can be bypassed.
