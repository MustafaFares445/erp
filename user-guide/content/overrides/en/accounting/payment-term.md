Payment terms guide due-date calculations and expectations on newly created documents. They are not a payment transaction or general-ledger posting. Review linked invoice or bill before changing a term; historical due dates and posted payments must not be rewritten simply by editing the shared rule. Permissions apply to creation and editing.
