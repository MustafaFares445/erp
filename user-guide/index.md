## Browse by module

<div class="module-grid">
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/sales/"><strong>Sales</strong><span>Sales controls the customer commercial lifecycle from quotation and confirmed demand through fulfillment, billing, collection and credit correction.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/accounting/"><strong>Accounting</strong><span>Accounting owns the financial truth of IERP: ledgers, receivables, payables, tax, periods, supplier liabilities and posted financial corrections.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/inventory/"><strong>Inventory</strong><span>Inventory owns physical stock truth: warehouses, quantities, tracked lots and serials, receipts, deliveries, transfers, counts, returns, reservations and replenishment.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/vendors/"><strong>Purchasing</strong><span>Purchasing manages supplier sourcing and commercial commitments while Inventory remains the only owner of physical receiving and Accounting owns supplier liabilities.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/crm/"><strong>CRM</strong><span>CRM organizes customers, leads, opportunities, interactions, campaigns and customer-specific commercial context before and around the sales process.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/employees/"><strong>Employees</strong><span>Employees covers workforce records, monthly planning, tasks, field visits, reviewed sales opportunities, performance and salary calculation.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/support/"><strong>Support and Maintenance</strong><span>Support & Maintenance coordinates customer tickets, SLA, warranty, service work, maintenance, field appointments, equipment context and advanced routing.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/reports/"><strong>Reports</strong><span>Reports provides consolidated operational and audit views. Reports explain business facts; corrections must be made in the module that owns the source record.</span></a>
<a class="module-card" href="/guide/screens/system/"><strong>Administration</strong><span>System contains access, templates and shared configuration surfaces used across the ERP.</span></a>
</div>

## How this guide is structured

Each screen article matches a real dashboard surface and explains navigation, purpose, registered pages, fields, table information, filters, actions, related records, business effects and common blocked states. Workflow articles connect those screens into complete end-to-end processes.

::: tip
Visible actions can vary by role and record state. If an option described in the guide is missing, check permissions and workflow prerequisites.
:::
