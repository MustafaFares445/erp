# Glossary

**Quotation** — a customer offer. It has no stock effect.

**Sales Order** — the confirmed customer demand created directly or from an accepted quotation.

**Fulfillment / Delivery** — the operational execution of what leaves the warehouse for the customer.

**Invoice** — the customer financial document for billed value.

**Credit Note** — a controlled credit against invoiced value.

**Purchase Order (PO)** — the commercial order sent to a supplier.

**Supplier Confirmation** — the supplier's committed quantity and promised date against a PO.

**Purchase Inbound** — the purchasing-side representation of expected receipt work.

**Inventory Receipt** — the physical receiving operation that changes warehouse stock when completed.

**Lot** — a batch identity shared by multiple units.

**Serial Number** — a unique identity for one tracked unit.

**FEFO** — First Expired, First Out; prioritizing stock with the earliest expiry when applicable.

**Accounts Receivable (AR)** — money customers owe the business.

**Accounts Payable (AP)** — money the business owes suppliers.

**Customer Deposit** — posted customer money not yet fully allocated to an invoice.

**Refund** — a financial repayment tied to an approved business credit/payment flow.

**SLA** — the service timing rules used to manage support response and resolution expectations.
